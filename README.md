# Anchor & Rebuild: Relational Trust Engine (Codename: ANCHOR)

Evidence-based clinical trust repair application for romantic couples recovering from severe relationship betrayals, chronic dishonesty, or infidelity.

## Theoretical Foundations
- **CBCT (Cognitive Behavioral Couple Therapy)**: Baucom 3-Stage Model (Impact/Crisis → Meaning-Making → Relational Redefinition).
- **EFT (Emotionally Focused Therapy)**: Johnson's Attachment Injury Resolution Model (AIRM) and Blamer-Softening Sequences.
- **Gottman Method**: Sound Relationship House, Atonement/Attunement/Attachment, and Physiological Flooding Management (>100 bpm Lockouts).
- **IBCT (Integrative Behavioral Couple Therapy)**: Emotional acceptance and unified detachment.
- **Operant Conditioning & Truth-Default Theory**: Reconditioning threat schemas through empirical, verified micro-behaviors.

6. **Module 5: Consensual Transparency Scaffolding & Sunset Manager**: Voluntary log feeds and automated 30-day sunset clause review to decommission surveillance.
7. **8-Phase Dynamic Clinical State Engine**: Stage-gated recovery progression.
8. **Psychometrics Suite**: 14-day Dyadic Trust Scale (DTS) and RDAS tracking.
9. **Safety Triage & Emergency Panic Disconnect**: IPV/coercive control and active deception hard-stop routing with instant data wipe.
